This boilerplate includes generic **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** pages with placeholder values. These pages are located at:

- `/terms-of-service` - Terms of Service
- `/privacy` - Privacy Policy

## Placeholder Values

The legal pages use placeholder values that you must replace with your actual company and service information before launching:

| Placeholder | Description | Example |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| `[COMPANY_NAME]` | Your legal entity name | Acme Inc. |
| `[APP_NAME]` | Your application/service name | MyApp |
| `[CONTACT_EMAIL]` | Support or legal contact email | legal@example.com |
| `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]` | Date the policy becomes effective | January 1, 2025 |
| `[JURISDICTION]` | Legal jurisdiction for disputes | State of Delaware, USA |
| `[WEBSITE_URL]` | Your main website URL | https://example.com |
| `[BRIEF_SERVICE_DESCRIPTION]` | Short description of your service | a platform for managing projects |

## Customizing the Pages

The legal pages are located in:

```
app/(marketing)/terms-of-service/page.tsx
app/(marketing)/privacy/page.tsx
```

### Option 1: Find and Replace

1. Open each file in your editor
2. Use Find and Replace (Cmd/Ctrl + H) to replace each placeholder
3. Review the content to ensure it matches your service

### Option 2: AI-Assisted Customization

You can use an AI assistant to help customize these pages:

```
Please update the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages with the following information:
- Company Name: [Your Company]
- App Name: [Your App]
- Contact Email: [Your Email]
- Jurisdiction: [Your Jurisdiction]
- Service Description: [Brief description of what your app does]
```

## Important Notice

These are **generic templates** and should be reviewed and customized before production use:

1. **Review all content** - Ensure the terms accurately reflect your service and business practices
2. **Consult a lawyer** - Have a legal professional review your policies, especially if you:
   - Handle sensitive user data
   - Process payments
   - Operate in multiple jurisdictions
   - Target specific industries with regulations (healthcare, finance, etc.)
3. **Keep policies updated** - Review and update your legal pages when you:
   - Add new features
   - Change data collection practices
   - Expand to new regions
   - Update third-party integrations

## File Structure

```
app/(marketing)/
├── terms-of-service/
│   └── page.tsx          # Terms of Service page
└── privacy/
    └── page.tsx          # Privacy Policy page

locales/index.ts          # Page metadata and titles
components/footer.tsx     # Footer with legal links
config/routes.ts          # Route definitions
```

## Routes Configuration

The legal routes are already configured in `config/routes.ts`:

```typescript
export const routes = {
  // ... other routes
  legal: {
    termsOfService: "/terms-of-service",
    privacy: "/privacy",
  },
};
```

These routes are whitelisted in the middleware, so they are accessible without authentication and even in landing mode.
